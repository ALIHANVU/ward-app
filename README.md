# Ward

**Ward** is an Islamic companion app for Android — prayer times, the Quran, hadith, duas and more, in one calm, modern app. Available in English, Russian and Chechen.

## Features

**Prayer times**
- Accurate prayer times for your location, with many calculation methods and manual adjustments
- Adhan notifications and full-screen adhan alarms with a choice of muezzins
- Current-prayer card with a countdown, the day's schedule, sunnah and forbidden-time markers
- Hijri calendar and monthly timetable
- Home-screen and lock-screen widgets

**Quran**
- Clear reader with Arabic text and published translations
- Classic 15-line mushaf view
- Audio recitation by many reciters, with word-by-word highlighting
- Tap any word to hear it and see its meaning and morphology
- Tajweed colouring, tafsir, bookmarks and personal notes

**Hadith, duas and adhkar**
- Major hadith collections with search and bookmarks
- Duas and morning/evening adhkar from Hisn al-Muslim
- Place-based dua reminders (home, mosque) and weather duas

**Search and assistant**
- Search across the Quran and hadith — by words or by meaning
- An AI assistant that answers with references to ayat, tafsir and hadith

**Qibla and mosques**
- Qibla compass
- Map of nearby mosques and prayer places

**Quran video editor**
- Create short videos with ayat, recitation, translations and backgrounds — ready to share

**Design**
- Glass interface with light and dark themes
- Optional Liquid Glass effects for capable phones

## Install

1. Open [Releases](https://github.com/ALIHANVU/ward-app/releases) and download the latest `Ward-*.apk`.
2. Open the file on your phone. If Android asks, allow installing from this source.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer on a 64-bit phone.
